Classification Regression

Learns easy-to-follow if/then decision rules from the dataset.

Best Used When
Your dataset contains mixed or non-linear patterns, and you want a model whose decisions are easier to explain.
Clustering

K-Means

Clustering

Groups similar dataset rows into clusters without requiring a prediction target for training.

Best Used When
You want to explore natural groups in numeric data. K-Means discovers clusters rather than predicting a known outcome.
New to these terms? Classification predicts a category, regression predicts a number, and clustering discovers groups without using a prediction target for training.
